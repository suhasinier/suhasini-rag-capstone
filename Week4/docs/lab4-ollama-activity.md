Week 4 — Ollama Activity
Ollama was integrated into the W4 pipeline as a third model option and the 10-question comparison completed successfully with llama3.2:3b at zero marginal API cost. The local llama3.2:3b model completed the 10 questions in 155.29 seconds, while the larger llama3.1:8b model completed them in 452.48 seconds, also at zero API cost. Streaming with llama3.2:3b produced a TTFT of 8.240 seconds and a total response time of 27.778 seconds. The offline privacy test also succeeded, confirming that the local model could answer a question while the internet was disconnected. The experiments showed that local models provide a useful zero-cost and privacy-friendly development option, although answer quality and latency can vary compared with hosted models.
