# Stakeholder Map

## 1. Primary User

Who exactly is the primary user?

Describe the role and the context in which the user will use the AI system.

> The primary user is a CBSE school teacher who needs to quickly find and understand information from CBSE/NCERT curriculum, assessment, learning-outcome, and education-framework documents while planning lessons and assessments. The AI assistant provides concise, document-grounded answers with source references where possible, reducing the need to search through multiple education documents manually.

## 2. Secondary Stakeholders

Who else has a stake in this system?

Include:
- Decision makers / sponsors
- At least one gatekeeper such as Security, Compliance, or Legal
- At least one role whose existing work will be augmented by the system

> Secondary stakeholders include school academic leadership or a curriculum coordinator who sponsors and evaluates the usefulness of the system; the school IT, data-privacy, or administration team who act as gatekeepers for appropriate system access and handling of education documents; subject teachers and curriculum/content staff whose existing work of searching, interpreting, and organizing curriculum information is augmented by the assistant; and learners/students who may also benefit from faster access to curriculum and assessment information.

## 3. Before-AI Workflow

How is the problem solved today without AI?

Describe the current process concretely, including the approximate time or effort involved.

> A teacher currently searches CBSE and NCERT websites, curriculum documents, PDFs, circulars, and assessment guidelines manually to answer curriculum-related questions. Finding the relevant document or section can take approximately 5–10 minutes per question, and another few minutes may be needed to read and interpret the relevant information. For a teacher handling several such questions during lesson planning or assessment preparation, this can amount to approximately 1–2 hours per week spent searching and interpreting information across documents.

## 4. Desired After-State

What should change after the AI system is introduced?

Describe the desired workflow and the value it should create for the primary user and stakeholders.

> The teacher can ask a natural-language question and receive a concise answer grounded in the relevant CBSE/NCERT documents, with source references where possible. Instead of manually searching multiple documents and interpreting information from different sections, the teacher can use the assistant as a first point of access and spend more time on lesson planning and assessment preparation. When sufficient supporting information is not available, the system should indicate uncertainty rather than invent an answer, supporting more reliable use of the information.

## 5. Top 3 Sponsor Metrics

What three measurable metrics will the sponsor use to judge whether the system is successful?

1. **Time to answer:** Reduce the average time required for a teacher to find and understand the information needed for a curriculum-related question, with a target of reducing the current ~5–10 minutes of document searching per question.

2. **Grounded answer quality:** Measure the percentage of evaluated answers that are accurate, grounded in the relevant CBSE/NCERT documents, and appropriately indicate uncertainty when sufficient evidence is unavailable.

3. **Teacher adoption:** Measure the number and percentage of target teachers who actively use the assistant for curriculum, learning-outcome, or assessment-related questions on a regular basis.

> Each metric should have a clear way to be measured.
