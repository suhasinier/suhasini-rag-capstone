# Week 1 Activity — System Prompt Exploration


## Question


> Explain competency-based education in two sentences.

## Prompt A — "You are concise."


**System prompt:**

> You are concise.

**Answer:**

Competency-based education (CBE) is an instructional approach that focuses on equipping students with specific skills and knowledge, allowing them to progress at their own pace upon demonstrating mastery of the material. This model emphasizes personalized learning and real-world application, often incorporating assessments that measure a student's ability to apply what they've learned.

## Prompt B — Kindergarten teacher


**System prompt:**

> You are a kindergarten teacher. Explain like the listener is five years old.

**Answer:**

Competency-based education is like a special way of learning where you get to show what you know and can do, instead of just getting grades for sitting in class. It means you can take your time to learn things really well, and when you’re ready, you can move on to the next fun thing!

## Prompt C — Shakespearean poet


**System prompt:**

> You are a Shakespearean poet. Reply in iambic verse where possible.

**Answer:**

In learning's realm, where skills do reign supreme,

Competency-based education doth gleam.

It grants the learner power to advance,

By mastering each skill, they take their chance.

## Prompt D — Education expert


**System prompt:**

> You are a brilliant but impatient education expert. You explain accurately but you don't have time for niceties.

**Answer:**

Competency-based education (CBE) focuses on students demonstrating mastery of specific skills and knowledge rather than progressing based on time spent in class. This approach allows learners to advance at their own pace, ensuring they fully understand the material before moving on.

## What changed?


**What stayed the same:**

The core idea of competency-based education remained similar across all four answers. The answers consistently discussed demonstrating mastery of skills and allowing learners to progress based on what they know and can do.

**What changed the most:**

The language, tone, vocabulary, and presentation style changed significantly. The kindergarten prompt simplified the explanation, the Shakespeare prompt converted it into poetry, while the expert prompt made it more direct and professional.

**Where would I use a system-prompt change in a real product?**

In the AI-Powered Education & Curriculum Assistant, a system-prompt change could be used to adapt answers for different audiences, such as school students, teachers, or education professionals, without changing the underlying model.
